Sources & scoring method

Loading employer index…

The collector checks these employer APIs twice daily. Discovery covers these feeds, not all of LinkedIn. Public-link imports read the posting you supply. Availability can change between checks.

Company priority uses selected, verified 2026 Fortune 500 US revenue ranks, with a source beside each rank. Coverage is partial: an unavailable rank does not mean a small or less desirable employer. Your priority list can promote companies such as SpaceX independently.

Skill overlap counts recognized skills once, with required mentions weighted twice. It does not judge skill proficiency, interviews, the applicant pool, or your chance of an offer. Pay is quoted from the posting; eligibility for a pay tier is not assumed.
