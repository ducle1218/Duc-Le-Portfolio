Geometry uses meters, Y up, and the cow faces negative X. The hoof bottoms lie
at Y = 0. The meshes have positions baked into their geometry, with no per-mesh
translation or rotation. Material patches and separate anatomical components
can be hidden or combined by the decorative contour renderer as needed.

## F-35 contour geometry

The simplified F-35-shaped background geometry is authored in this project.
It uses the U.S. Air Force's public visual reference for general aircraft shape:
https://www.af.mil/News/Art/igphoto/2000790758/

No external F-35 mesh, CAD model, texture, or downloaded illustration is included.
The procedural geometry is a visual approximation for the portfolio background,
not an engineering model of the aircraft.

## Airflow visualization

The animated airflow uses an analytic, illustrative flow field to suggest a
wind-tunnel visualization. It is not validated computational fluid dynamics
(CFD), and it does not represent measured or predicted aerodynamic performance
of either the aircraft or the cow.
