PROBLEM STATEMENT

VEX High Stakes comes down to scoring rings on stakes fast and grabbing mobile goals cleanly. Our earlier robots kept letting us down — loose wheels, a conveyor belt that slipped, no way to self-intake rings, and motor calibration that was off just enough to send the robot veering off course.

KEY REQUIREMENTS

  • ✓ Ground-level automatic ring pickup
  • ✓ Mobile Goal Capture (MGC) clamping hold
  • ✓ Score 12+ points in skills trials

Engineering Notebook & Timeline Progress

Chronological record of VEX High Stakes design milestones and testing results

January 20 — 21, 2025

Game Plan Strategy & Drivetrain Design

Our core strategy focused on building and testing each individual subsystem in isolation before final assembly. We opted for a simple 4-wheel clawbot drivetrain with no changes to ensure maximum stability and minimize troubleshooting efforts.

Design Sketch Details: Conveyor belt system lifts rings up to 17 inches, transporting and sliding them onto mobile goals, held together by an elastic rubber band mechanism.
January 23 — 24, 2025

Ground-Intake & Mobile Goal Capture Subsystems

Remodeled the initial intake to support direct ground pickups, eliminating manual loading. Conveyor lifts rings 17" high. Added guide wheels to prevent rollbacks.

Mobile Goal Capture (MGC): Added a horizontal shaft bracket below the conveyor to lock the goal base at a stable angle and prevent conveyor jams.
January 25 — 31, 2025

7 Match Trials & Mathematical Performance Analysis

We conducted 7 match trials to measure consistency. In Trial 6, we reached peak performance, scoring 12 points with fast pickups and smooth ring loading!

✓ Average score when scoring was 6.5 points, demonstrating consistent development.
