Project Overview

I was lead designer on this EGR 101 launch vehicle project through Embry-Riddle — I handled the core math, trajectory sims, and structural analysis for the team. The goal was a medium-lift, two-stage orbital rocket (we named it "The Crayon") that could carry a 4,500 lb satellite payload safely into low Earth orbit.

I ran the structural calcs to make sure the airframe could survive max dynamic pressure (Max-Q). That meant landing on a 1/16-inch skin in Aluminum 6063-T5, with bulkheads made from a lightweight, high-strength Titanium Matrix Composite (TMC).

Core Calculations & Mathematical Model

  • Staging Optimization: Formulated propulsion staging parameters in MATLAB to select a solid Castor-120 booster for Stage 1 (providing 394,000 lbs of thrust) coupled with a high-efficiency liquid oxygen/hydrogen J-2 vacuum engine for Stage 2.
  • Vehicle Proportions: Defined a 125 ft vehicle concept with a 10.35 ft diameter for the preliminary sizing study.
  • Trajectory Modeling: Handled the delta-V orbital trajectory budget, mapping rocket thrust-to-weight transitions and fuel burning profiles to reach a target LEO altitude of 102.83 Nautical Miles.
